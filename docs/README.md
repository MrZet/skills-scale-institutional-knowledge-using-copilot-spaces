# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

## Project management processes

OctoAcme manages projects across the full lifecycle: teams initiate work by defining the problem, desired outcomes, stakeholders, and success measures, then plan scope, milestones, estimates, dependencies, and quality expectations. During execution, cross-functional teams deliver small increments, track work on a project board, communicate progress and blockers through regular check-ins, and maintain a risk register. Releases follow validation and deployment checks, with rollback plans and stakeholder updates; retrospectives turn lessons from sprints, releases, and incidents into owned improvement actions. The process is supported by clear roles—including the Project Manager, Product Manager/Product Lead, developers, QA/testing, and stakeholders—and emphasizes customer value, data-informed decisions, and continuous improvement.
