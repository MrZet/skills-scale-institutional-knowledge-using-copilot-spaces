# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises. A project may combine roles on smaller efforts, but should assign each responsibility and decision clearly. Role names may vary across organizations.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with project and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Business Owner / Sponsor

### Role Summary
The Business Owner or Sponsor is accountable for the business case, organizational backing, and intended outcomes of the project. This role provides direction and resolves decisions that exceed the project team's authority.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm the business need, expected outcomes, and success measures
- Secure appropriate resources and organizational support
- Make or delegate major funding and scope decisions
- Resolve escalations that the Project Manager cannot settle within agreed authority
- Champion the project with impacted groups and leadership

### Interactions and Decision Boundaries
- Partners with the Product Manager to align product outcomes and priorities with business goals
- Agrees governance and escalation paths with the Project Manager, who coordinates delivery but does not replace the Sponsor's organizational authority
- Consults the Product Owner and Developers on user and technical impacts before major decisions
- Delegates day-to-day product decisions where appropriate; remains accountable for sponsorship and business outcomes

---

## Product Owner / Business Representative

### Role Summary
The Product Owner or Business Representative speaks for users and business stakeholders in day-to-day delivery, translating needs into clear requirements and confirming that completed work meets those needs. This role complements the Product Manager; one person may perform both roles when appropriate.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and clarify user and stakeholder needs
- Maintain and refine requirements or the delivery backlog with the Product Manager
- Define and clarify acceptance criteria
- Provide timely answers to business questions and review delivered work against agreed criteria
- Surface stakeholder impacts, constraints, and changes in business priorities

### Interactions and Decision Boundaries
- Works with the Product Manager on product direction, backlog priority, and outcome measures; the Product Manager retains product-level prioritization accountability unless explicitly delegated
- Coordinates with the Project Manager on timing, dependencies, and scope implications
- Collaborates with Developers to clarify requirements and review increments
- Confirms acceptance against agreed criteria, while escalating changes to agreed scope or business outcomes to the appropriate Product Manager or Sponsor

---

## Technical Lead / Solution Architect

### Role Summary
The Technical Lead or Solution Architect guides the technical approach and helps ensure the solution is secure, maintainable, and feasible within project constraints.

### Responsibilities
- Guide architecture, design choices, and technical standards
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and trade-offs; recommend mitigations
- Support estimates and sequencing for technical work
- Review implementation for alignment with agreed design and quality expectations
- Communicate technical constraints and operational considerations to the project team

### Interactions and Decision Boundaries
- Partners with Developers on implementation and technical reviews
- Coordinates with the Project Manager on technical dependencies, estimates, schedule impacts, and risk escalation
- Advises the Product Manager and Product Owner about technical trade-offs that affect user value or scope
- Makes or recommends technical decisions within delegated authority; escalates material changes in scope, cost, security, or risk through project governance

---

## Project Coordinator / PMO Support

### Role Summary
The Project Coordinator or PMO Support role provides administrative and process support so project information, actions, and decisions remain visible and current. This role supports, but does not assume, the Project Manager's accountability for delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain schedules, project boards, action items, and decision logs as delegated
- Prepare status materials and coordinate meeting logistics and follow-ups
- Track due dates and surface overdue actions, dependencies, and process gaps
- Help keep project documentation organized and accessible

### Interactions and Decision Boundaries
- Works under the direction of the Project Manager and promptly flags changes or blockers for their attention
- Collects updates from Developers, Product roles, and other contributors without making their decisions for them
- May maintain records and communicate approved information, but does not independently change scope, priorities, commitments, or risk responses

---

## Change and Adoption Lead

### Role Summary
The Change and Adoption Lead helps people and operations prepare for, adopt, and sustain the change delivered by a project.

### Responsibilities
- Assess stakeholder impacts and readiness
- Plan communications, engagement, training, and adoption support
- Coordinate readiness activities and identify adoption risks
- Define or track adoption measures with business stakeholders
- Gather feedback after release and communicate lessons to the project team

### Interactions and Decision Boundaries
- Partners with the Sponsor on visible sponsorship and stakeholder alignment
- Coordinates with the Project Manager to integrate readiness, communications, and training activities into the plan and surface risks
- Works with the Product Owner or Business Representative to understand user needs and with Developers to explain solution changes and constraints
- Recommends adoption activities and reports readiness; release or scope decisions remain with the designated project governance and accountable owners

---

## Role Assignment and Collaboration

Not every project needs a separate person for every role. On smaller projects, one person may cover multiple roles (for example, Product Manager and Product Owner, or Project Manager and Project Coordinator) if conflicts of interest and decision authority remain clear. A person should not be treated as accountable for a decision merely because they maintain its documentation or coordinate the work.

At project kickoff, agree who is responsible, accountable, consulted, and informed for key decisions and handoffs. In particular, clarify who approves scope changes, owns risk responses and escalations, confirms acceptance, and assesses release readiness. The Sponsor should resolve escalations beyond delegated authority; the Project Manager coordinates the decision process and communicates its delivery impact.
